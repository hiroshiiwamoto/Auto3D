3D Car Design Tool

Sketch car designs in 3D like drawing with pencil, but with full volumetric freedom. Draw profile curves, cross-sections, and generate surfaces.

1. Press 2 for side view
2. Press D to draw, click points
3. Press Enter to finish curve
4. Draw cross-sections in front view 3
5. Select all + click Loft to make surface
